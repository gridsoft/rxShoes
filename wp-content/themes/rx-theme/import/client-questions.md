## 2. Product descriptions are copied from TWL (duplicate content)

All product descriptions (and SEO titles/descriptions) were taken from **twl.com.au**. Two things follow from that:

- **Search ranking.** Google sees text that already exists on another site as duplicate content and usually ranks the original (TWL) above the copy. This won't break anything at launch, but **we recommend rewriting the descriptions in RX Shoes' own words over time**, starting with the best sellers.
- **TWL-specific text was removed.** Some descriptions contained paragraphs that only apply to TWL. We removed them rather than changing "TWL" to "RX Shoes", because they describe TWL's own policies and dealer status, which may not be true for RX Shoes:

| Product(s) | Removed text (summary) |
|---|---|
| Vivobarefoot Primus Trail FG 3.5 (Men's), Motus Flex (Women's, Obsidian) | "Sent by a marketplace partner… excluded from free gift and TWL App offers… contact support@twl.com.au" |
| LUXIAOJUN: Weightlifting Shoes (Blue), Nature Trainers 1.0 and 2.0, Sports Slides, X-Load (Black and White), PowerPro (Red and White), CrossFit PowerPro (Ranger Green and Pukie Green) | "Can't be returned through the TWL Returns Portal… contact the TWL support team" and delivery ETA notes |
| R.A.D ONE V2, R.A.D SYNTH | "TWL is Australia's only Authorised R.A.D® Dealer…" |

**Please let us know:**
- Is RX Shoes an authorised R.A.D dealer? If so, we can add an equivalent line.
- Do any LUXIAOJUN or Vivobarefoot items have special delivery or return conditions at RX Shoes that should be stated on those products?

Also removed: the "– TWL" at the end of every SEO title, the TWL internal tags (e.g. "SALE11", "credit_fee:0") and 24 size-guide/banner images that were loaded directly from TWL's website.

## 4. Set automatically, please review when convenient

- **Stock:** the stock numbers from the list are used for every colour and size (1,112 in stock, 985 sold out). Sold-out sizes are greyed out for that colour, and stock goes down with each order. One product is currently sold out in every size. **Please confirm the stock numbers are current**, and let us know how stock will be kept up to date after launch (updated in the website admin, or re-imported from a new list).
- **Bundle offer:** **all** products are eligible for the 2-pair / 3-pair bundle discount.
- **"Best for" and shoe type** (used in the shop filters and product cards): set automatically from the product names (Training, Weightlifting, Running, Barefoot, Recovery, HYROX). They can be changed per product in the admin.
- **Men's / Women's / Unisex** categories: taken from the product names and size charts.
- **Product names:** where a product comes in several colours, the single colour at the end of the name was removed (e.g. "Reebok – Nano X5 Edge – Women's"), since shoppers pick the colour on the page. Single-colour products keep their colour in the name.
